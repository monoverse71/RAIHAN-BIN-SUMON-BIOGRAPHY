The Vision

A refined residential landmark, designed for elevated everyday living.

BONOFUL TOWER is envisioned as a refined residential landmark in Mymensingh, designed to bring elevated urban living, thoughtful planning and contemporary comfort together in one distinguished address. Rising 17 storeys along the Dhaka–Mymensingh Highway, it is a residence created for families who value location, space, comfort, modern lifestyle, accessibility and long-term value.
