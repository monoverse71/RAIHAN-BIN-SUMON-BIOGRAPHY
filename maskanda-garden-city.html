The Vision

A modern residential address, built for the advancement of Mymensingh living.

MASKANDA GARDEN CITY is a modern residential development designed around comfortable family living, connectivity, security, greenery and lifestyle amenities. Rising 16 storeys in Maskanda, it is positioned for people who want to enjoy a better, more comfortable, modern lifestyle — with convenient access to main roads, Maskanda Bus Stand, Mymensingh Medical College, the railway station, a nearby mosque, schools and colleges, and shopping and market facilities.
