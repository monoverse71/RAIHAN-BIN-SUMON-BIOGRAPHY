The Master Concept

Two buildings, thoughtfully connected — never merged.

Brahmaputra Arch Lake View is planned as a premium mixed-use landmark near the Brahmaputra River — combining a separate 7-storey commercial building with a separate 20-storey premium residential tower. Each building stands as its own distinct architectural structure, together forming a single, cohesive address defined by modern design, everyday convenience and riverside living.
